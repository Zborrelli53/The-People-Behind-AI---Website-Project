The People Behind Artificial Intelligence

(Based on: "AI Documentary: or How I became an Apocaloptamist")

Who are some big names behind Artificial Intelligence? What are their stances on AI? Are they pessimistic, positive, excited, scared, or somewhere in between?

Brief Introduciton

The "AI Documentary: or How I became an Apocaloptamist", what is it?

The Documentary was a project created by Daniel Roher and Charlie Tyrell

The Film Dives into the thoughts and emotions about Artificial Intelligence in the near future. It is a quest to find out whether we should be scared or excited for the future of AI.

Discussed various questions such as "Is it a good time to have a child?", "Will AI bring a sort of doomsday upon us?" Etc.

Meaning of "Apocoloptamist"

A person or being that expects a major crisis or event, but can still remain positive about the future.
