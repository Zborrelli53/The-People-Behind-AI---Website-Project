The People Behind Artificial Intelligence

(Based on: "AI Documentary: or How I became an Apocaloptamist")

Who are some big names behind Artificial Intelligence? What are their stances on AI? Are they pessimistic, positive, excited, scared, or somewhere in between?

Brief Introduciton

The "AI Documentary: or How I became an Apocaloptamist", what is it?

  • The Documentary was a project created by Daniel Roher and Charlie Tyrell
  • The Film Dives into the thoughts and emotions about Artificial Intelligence in the near future. It is a quest to find out whether we should be scared or excited for the future of AI.
  • Discussed various questions such as "Is it a good time to have a child?", "Will AI bring a sort of doomsday upon us?" Etc.

Meaning of "Apocoloptamist"

"A person or being that expects a major crisis or event, but can still remain positive about the future"

Shane Legg

Shane is the Co-Founder of the AI lab at DeepMind

He is known for his public advocacy regarding existential risk from AI and has signed statements highlighting the importance of AI safety.

He has received the Digital Masters Award for Excellence in Technology.

Shane Legg is cautious and worried about the advanceing AI and AGI

Eliezer Yudkowsky

Eliezer is a founding researcher & co-founder of the Machine Intelligence Research Institute

Founded the Community Blog "LessWrong" in 2009, where he has written hundreds of articles on the dangers of Artificial Intelligence

Helped to introduce the topic of AI extinction risk to mainstream audiences such as X (Formerly known as twitter), TikTok, Youtube, and more

Elizer is one of the major figures who preaches the dangers of AI. Overall Pessimistic.

Deborah Raji

Deborah is a researcher and advocate dedicated to addressing the bias of Artificial Intelligence

Deborah Raji is a member of the Mozilla foundation. She collaborates with others at the foundation to improve the transparency and accountability of AI

Deborah Is an Advisory Board Member for the Center for Democracy and Technology AI Governance Lab, the Health AI Partnership, REALML and the Center for Civil Rights and Technology​

Overall Worried about the future of Artificial Intelligence

Nitasha Tiku

Tiku is a Tech Culture Reporter at The Washington Post in San Francisco

She has spent over a decade covering Silicon Valley and technology culture, working as a senior writer for WIRED.

Spearheaded extensive journalistic coverage tracking how Silicon Valley trends—such as generative AI and AI companions effect human relationships and consumer psychology.

Skeptical and Concerend about the future of Artificial Intelligence

Peter Diamandis

Peter is the founder and executive chairman of XPRIZE

Co-Founded Singularity University, Ling Exponential Adventures, and BOLD Adventures​

Has launched over $600 Million in competitions driving more than $10 billion in research and development across space, health, robotics, climate, quantum and AI.

Has a positive outlook in AI,and plays a major role in the development of AI
